Before you lock in dates: AIB-C01 is a brand-new AWS certification. The official exam guide documents 130 minutes and 700/1000 as the passing score, but AWS is also currently offering a beta version of 85 questions and 170 minutes for USD 50. Check your AWS Certification account to see which version applies to you before scheduling the exam; this plan is generic (week 1 through week 5) so you can adapt it to your own date.

Week-by-week plan
Week 1 — Domain 1: AI Fundamentals and Literacy (24%)
1.1
Basic concepts and terminology
AI vs ML vs GenAI, training vs inference, structured vs unstructured data, why data quality matters, global frameworks such as ISO/IEC 23053 and 42001.
1.2
Choosing the right type of AI solution
Rules-based automation vs AI, what distinguishes an AI agent (autonomy, tool use, agent-to-agent communication), continuous monitoring and drift, shadow AI classification.
1.3
GenAI concepts and techniques
Basic prompt engineering, context window and token limits, RAG vs fine-tuning to adapt a model to business needs.

Week 2 — Domain 2: AI Strategy and Business Value Creation (28%, the heaviest)
2.1
AI strategies aligned to the business
Mapping high-impact use cases, build vs buy vs partner decisions, prioritizing initiatives (scale, pause, terminate), recognizing when AI is not the solution, evaluating transitions between solutions.
2.2
Measuring and demonstrating business value
Tangible and intangible KPIs, baselines before implementation, ROI calculation with comprehensive frameworks, early success indicators, basic cost control.
2.3
Positioning AI as a competitive advantage
Assessing the competitive landscape, transforming business models with AI, sustainable advantages, investment level based on industry maturity.

Week 3 — Domain 3: AI Governance and Responsible AI Leadership (24%)
3.1
Principles of responsible AI
Fairness, explainability, privacy, safety, transparency, robustness. Tensions between business goals and these principles. When human oversight is needed: hallucination detection, guardrails, escalation criteria.
3.2
Governance and regulatory compliance
Cross-functional structures with clear accountability, regulatory risks of AI-driven processes, access controls and data security, lifecycle-based risk classification frameworks.
3.3
Enterprise risks and mitigation
Controls and monitoring in production, bias across multiple lifecycle stages and bias drift, harmful content and intellectual property, hallucinations and model drift.

Week 4 — Domain 4: Business Readiness, Leadership and AI Transformation (24%)
4.1
Maturity and readiness to adopt AI
Readiness dimensions (leadership, data, culture, infrastructure, governance), AI maturity models, capability gaps, investment prioritization.
4.2
Data foundations and infrastructure
Data quality and accessibility, data silos, data strategy and ownership, foundational infrastructure requirements.
4.3
Leading change and workforce capabilities
Executive sponsorship, cross-functional teams, transparent communication, cultural barriers, development programs (POC, hackathons), transition of human roles.
4.4
Scaling from pilots to enterprise deployments
Envision/experiment/launch/scale phases, early wins, AI centers of excellence (COE), continuous feedback, transition from experimental to production.
